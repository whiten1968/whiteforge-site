Opening Research Studio privacy policy

For the Opening Research Studio app (iPad & Mac) · Last updated 23 November 2026

Summary. Opening Research Studio stores your research, games, collections and training on your device and in your own iCloud account, which Apple syncs privately between your iPad and Mac. None of it is sent to Whiteforge — there are no Whiteforge servers in the data path. The app contains no analytics, no telemetry and no advertising. Engine analysis runs on your device. The only other outbound connections happen when you ask for them: to Lichess or Chess.com to import your games, to the Lichess Masters database during analysis, and to The Week in Chess when you build a reference database.

1. Introduction

This policy explains how Opening Research Studio handles your data. Opening Research Studio is an application for iPad and Mac published by Whiteforge Technologies Ltd (“Whiteforge”, “we”), a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17224707). It is a workspace for studying chess openings: research guides, engine and database analysis, your own games, training and study materials.

2. Data Whiteforge receives

None. Whiteforge operates no servers for this app and receives no personal data, usage data or analytics from it. Your research and games are not transmitted to us. We cannot see your library, your games or your activity.

3. What is stored on your device

Your work is stored locally on your device: your research projects, your games database (including any games you import), the engine analysis and model games you save, your collections, and your training cards and schedule. This data stays on your device except insofar as you choose to use iCloud sync (below).

4. iCloud sync

If you are signed into iCloud, your games, collections and training sync between your own devices using Apple’s CloudKit, in the private database of your personal iCloud account. This means:

  • The data is stored in your own iCloud account, managed by Apple, and is governed by Apple’s privacy policy and your iCloud terms.
  • It is used only to keep your own devices in step. It is not shared with other users, and it is not accessible to Whiteforge — we have no access to your private iCloud database.
  • You control it through your Apple account. Signing out of iCloud, or disabling iCloud for the app, stops syncing; deleting the app’s iCloud data removes it from the cloud.

5. Importing your games (Lichess & Chess.com)

When you ask the app to import your games, it connects directly to the public Lichess or Chess.com API for the username you enter and downloads those games to your device. Your username is sent to that service only to make the request. Whiteforge is not involved in, and does not receive, that exchange.

6. Reference databases (The Week in Chess)

When you build a reference database, the app downloads weekly game archives directly from The Week in Chess (theweekinchess.com) to your device. No personal data is sent beyond an ordinary web request. You may also import your own PGN files or databases, which are read entirely on your device.

7. Engine analysis & the Masters database

Engine analysis runs entirely on your device (Stockfish on Mac, a built-in engine on iPad); positions are not sent anywhere to be analysed. When you use the Masters feature, the app queries the public Lichess Masters opening database for the position on the board; only the position (not any personal data) is sent.

8. Network activity

The app makes outbound network connections only for the actions above, and only when you initiate them: importing your games (Lichess / Chess.com), building a reference database (The Week in Chess), the Masters database during analysis (Lichess), and iCloud sync and App Store purchases (Apple). There is no background telemetry.

9. Third parties

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. The third-party services it can contact, at your request, are Apple (iCloud/CloudKit and the App Store), Lichess, Chess.com and The Week in Chess. Each is governed by its own privacy policy.

10. Children

Opening Research Studio is a general-audience study tool. It does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children, because it collects no personal data for Whiteforge at all.

11. Your rights

Because Whiteforge holds no personal data about you in connection with this app, there is nothing for us to access, correct or erase. You remain in full control of the data on your device and in your iCloud account, which you can export or delete at any time using the app and your Apple account.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the “Last updated” date above. Material changes will be summarised at the top of the policy for a reasonable period.
